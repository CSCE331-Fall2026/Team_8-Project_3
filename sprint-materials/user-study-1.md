# User Study 1

## Study Goals
- Gauge UI quality
- Improve usability of product

## Questions Asked
### Likert-scale
1. The user interface was quick and easy to use. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree
2. The user interface was visually pleasing. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree
3. The user interface had everything I needed to accomplish my goals. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree

### Open-ended / interview
1. Did everything get added properly?
2. Did anything appear that shouldn't have?
3. Did anything take too long, or did any errors occur?

#### CUSTOMER QUESTIONS
Try buying an orange chicken combo, with rice.
1. How easy was it to find what you needed?
2. How good did it feel for you to order?
3. Was there anything that felt like it impeded on the experience?

#### CASHIER QUESTIONS
Try making an order consisting of 5 items.
1. Did you feel like you had everything you want to order?
2. Did anything feel pointless or redundant?
3. Was anything overwhelming or visually cluttered?

## Participant Results & Observations
